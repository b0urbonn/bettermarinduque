# Receive Relief Goods or Cash Aid During Disasters

How disaster-affected families in Marinduque receive relief goods and cash assistance — and how to make sure you are on the list.

## Who can use this

Families affected by typhoons, floods, fires, or other disasters and emergencies in Marinduque.

## Where to go

- **Your barangay hall** — the barangay lists affected families and distributes the first wave of relief.
- **MSWDO (Municipal Hall)** — municipal relief distribution and cash aid.
- **PSWDO** — Capitol Compound, Brgy. Santol, Boac, for province-coordinated relief.
- **DSWD** — **AICS** (Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations) can provide cash aid for disaster, medical, and other crises; accessed through the welfare offices.
- **MDRRMO / PDRRMO** — for evacuation and disaster reporting. PDRRMO: 0968-226-5727; Santa Cruz MDRRMO: 0920-455-4034 (verify before relying; if unreachable, dial 911).

## What to bring

- [ ] Valid ID and proof of residence in the affected area
- [ ] Barangay certification of being affected (damage report, fire certification, etc.)
- [ ] Photos of damage, if available

## Steps

1. **Report the damage** to your barangay immediately after the disaster so your household gets on the affected-families list — relief is distributed by list.
2. If evacuated, register at the evacuation center with the camp managers.
3. For cash aid (AICS) or additional assistance, apply at the MSWDO or the designated payout venue announced by the LGU or DSWD.
4. Keep your certifications and receipts; requirements are usually checked at distribution.

## Fees

Relief goods and government cash aid are free — never pay anyone to get on a list.
