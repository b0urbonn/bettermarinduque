# Apply for Senior Citizen, Solo Parent, or PWD Assistance

How to get IDs and assistance for senior citizens, solo parents, and persons with disabilities in Marinduque.

## Who can use this

- **Senior citizens** — residents 60 years old and above
- **Solo parents** — those raising children alone under the Solo Parents Welfare Act
- **Persons with disabilities (PWDs)** — residents with physical, sensory, intellectual, or psychosocial disabilities

## Where to go

- **OSCA (Office for Senior Citizens Affairs)** — at your Municipal Hall, for the senior citizen ID and purchase booklets.
- **PDAO (Persons with Disability Affairs Office)** — at your Municipal Hall, for the PWD ID.
- **MSWDO** — at your Municipal Hall, for solo parent IDs and all welfare assistance.
- **PSWDO (Provincial Social Welfare and Development Office)** — Capitol Compound, Brgy. Santol, Boac, for province-level programs.
- **DSWD** — national programs: **4Ps** (conditional cash transfer for poor households) and **AICS** (crisis assistance). Apply through your MSWDO.

## What to bring

- [ ] Birth certificate or any document proving age (seniors) — or medical certificate/abstract (PWDs) — or documents proving solo parent status
- [ ] Barangay certificate of residency
- [ ] Valid ID and 1x1 or 2x2 photos
- [ ] For assistance requests: certificate of indigency

## Steps

1. Go to the right desk at your Municipal Hall (OSCA / PDAO / MSWDO).
2. Fill out the registration form and submit the documents.
3. Claim your ID — senior and PWD IDs give statutory discounts and VAT exemption on medicines, food, transport, and services.
4. Ask the MSWDO about supplementary programs — social pension for indigent seniors, assistive devices, and livelihood aid are administered through the welfare offices and DSWD.

## Fees

IDs for seniors, solo parents, and PWDs are issued free of charge.
