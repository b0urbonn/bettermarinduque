# Join Livelihood or Skills Training Programs

Skills and livelihood programs available to Marinduque residents — through TESDA, DOLE/PESO, the welfare offices, and DTI.

## Who can use this

Unemployed or underemployed residents, out-of-school youth, women and solo parents seeking income, and anyone wanting new skills.

## Where to go

- **PESO (Public Employment Service Office)** — at your Municipal Hall. Job referrals, job fairs, and DOLE programs.
- **TESDA** — free or subsidized technical-vocational courses; ask at PESO or TESDA's provincial office for schedules.
- **MSWDO / PSWDO** — livelihood assistance and sustainable livelihood programs run through the welfare offices.
- **DTI Marinduque** — livelihood and enterprise development assistance for small businesses.
- **Local schools** — Buyabod School of Arts and Trades (Santa Cruz) and Torrijos Poblacion School of Arts and Trades run technical courses; MarSU also runs skills and extension programs.

## What to bring

- [ ] Valid ID and barangay certificate of residency
- [ ] Filled-out registration form
- [ ] For livelihood grants: a simple project or business proposal, if required

## Steps

1. Visit your PESO desk or MSWDO and ask which trainings or livelihood programs are open.
2. Register and attend the training; keep your certificate.
3. For livelihood aid, submit the required proposal or undergo assessment by the welfare office.
4. Some programs provide starter kits or capital assistance after training — ask about post-training support.

## Fees

Government trainings are generally free; ask the organizer about any materials contribution.
