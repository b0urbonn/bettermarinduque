# Get Family and Community Welfare Support

Welfare services for families in Marinduque — counseling, assistance for children and women, daycare, and crisis aid through the MSWDO/PSWDO and DSWD.

## Who can use this

Families and individuals needing welfare support — parents in crisis, women and children needing protection, indigent families, and communities needing social services.

## Where to go

- **MSWDO** — at your Municipal Hall. The frontline office for family and community welfare: counseling referrals, assistance for women and children, daycare (Child Development Centers), and crisis aid.
- **PSWDO** — Capitol Compound, Brgy. Santol, Boac. Province-level social welfare programs.
- **DSWD** — national programs: **4Ps** cash transfers for poor households with children, and **AICS** crisis assistance. Access through your MSWDO.
- **Barangay** — Barangay Council for the Protection of Children and VAWC desk for protection concerns; for VAWC emergencies, call the PNP station or 911.

## What to bring

- [ ] Valid ID and barangay certificate
- [ ] Certificate of indigency for assistance programs
- [ ] Case documents if seeking protection (blotter, medical certificate, etc.)

## Steps

1. Go to your MSWDO and describe your situation — a social worker will assess and route you to the right service.
2. For protection concerns, report to the barangay VAWC desk or the PNP Women's and Children's Protection Desk.
3. For cash or material aid, complete the assessment and documentary requirements; releasing follows the program's schedule.

## Fees

Social welfare services are free.
