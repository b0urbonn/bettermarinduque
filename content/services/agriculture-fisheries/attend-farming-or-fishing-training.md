# Attend Farming or Fishing Training

Training programs for farmers and fisherfolk in Marinduque — from OPAg, PVET, TESDA, DA-MIMAROPA, BFAR, and local schools.

## Who can use this

Farmers, fisherfolk, youth, and anyone who wants skills training in agriculture, livestock, or fisheries.

## Where to go

- **Office of the Provincial Agriculturist (OPAg)** — Capitol Compound, Brgy. Santol, Boac. Runs farmer training and extension services.
- **Provincial Veterinary Office (PVET)** — Capitol Compound, Boac, for livestock-raising training.
- **Municipal Agriculture Office** — at your Municipal Hall, for local training schedules.
- **TESDA** — national skills training programs; ask your Municipal Hall's PESO desk or TESDA's provincial office for course offerings.
- **DA-MIMAROPA and BFAR** — regional agriculture and fisheries agencies that run trainings in the province.
- **Marinduque State University (MarSU)** — agriculture programs at the Torrijos campus and fisheries at the Gasan campus; tel (042) 332-2028. Also relevant: Buyabod School of Arts and Trades (Santa Cruz) and Torrijos Poblacion School of Arts and Trades.

## What to bring

- [ ] Valid ID and proof of residency
- [ ] Filled-out registration form for the specific training

## Steps

1. Ask your Municipal Agriculture Office or OPAg what trainings are scheduled.
2. Register through the organizing office or your barangay.
3. Attend the sessions — some trainings issue certificates useful for livelihood or employment.

## Fees

Government-run trainings are usually free; ask the organizer whether there is any fee or required contribution (meals, materials).
