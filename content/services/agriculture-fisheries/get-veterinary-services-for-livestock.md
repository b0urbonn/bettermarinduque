# Get Veterinary Services for Livestock

Veterinary services for livestock and farm animals in Marinduque — through the Provincial Veterinary Office and your municipal agriculture office.

## Who can use this

Livestock and poultry raisers, and pet owners needing government veterinary services such as vaccination and treatment.

## Where to go

- **Provincial Veterinary Office (PVET)** — Capitol Compound, Brgy. Santol, Boac. Handles animal health services and programs for the province.
- **Municipal Agriculture Office** — at your Municipal Hall; coordinates local vaccination drives (e.g., anti-rabies) and livestock services.

Check the [Provincial Citizen's Charter](https://marinduque.gov.ph/citizens-charter/) for the requirements and processing steps for PVET services.

## What to bring

- [ ] Valid ID and proof of ownership/residency
- [ ] Animal health or vaccination records, if available
- [ ] For large animals: be ready to describe the herd/flock size and condition

## Steps

1. Ask your barangay or Municipal Agriculture Office about scheduled vaccination or treatment days in your area.
2. For individual cases (sick livestock, emergencies, artificial insemination requests), contact PVET at the Capitol or through your municipal agriculture office.
3. Bring the animal or arrange a field visit as instructed.

## Fees

Routine government veterinary services and mass vaccination drives are generally free or low-cost — confirm current fees with PVET or your municipal office.
