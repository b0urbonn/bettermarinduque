# Ask for Free Seeds, Seedlings, Fertilizers, or Fingerlings

How farmers and fisherfolk in Marinduque can request free planting materials and fingerlings from the province and national agencies.

Agriculture is a major part of Marinduque's economy — coconut, rice (palay), banana, corn, and root crops, plus fishing. Free inputs are distributed through the **Office of the Provincial Agriculturist (OPAg)**, municipal agriculture offices, and national agencies.

## Who can use this

Registered farmers, fisherfolk, and community or school gardening groups in Marinduque.

## Where to go

- **Office of the Provincial Agriculturist (OPAg)** — Capitol Compound, Brgy. Santol, Boac. Handles provincial agriculture extension and input distribution.
- **Your Municipal Agriculture Office** — at your Municipal Hall; municipal offices run their own distribution and keep farmer registries.
- **DA-MIMAROPA** (Department of Agriculture regional office) — for national distribution programs reaching the province.
- **BFAR** — for fingerlings and fisheries inputs (fisherfolk).

See the [Provincial Citizen's Charter](https://marinduque.gov.ph/citizens-charter/) for the official steps and requirements for OPAg services.

## What to bring

- [ ] Valid ID and proof that you farm or fish in the municipality (barangay certification or RSBSA/farmer registry enrollment)
- [ ] Request letter or filled-out form, if the office requires one

## Steps

1. Make sure you are registered in your municipality's farmer or fisherfolk registry — ask your barangay or Municipal Agriculture Office about RSBSA enrollment.
2. Watch for distribution announcements from OPAg, your municipality, or the Provincial Government's Facebook page (facebook.com/provincialgovernmentofmarinduque).
3. Submit your request at OPAg or the Municipal Agriculture Office with your ID and registry details.
4. Collect the inputs on the announced schedule.

## Fees

Government-distributed farm and fishery inputs are free.
