# Borrow Farm Tools or Equipment from the LGU

How to request the use of government farm tools and machinery in Marinduque — through OPAg and your municipal agriculture office.

## Who can use this

Registered farmers, farmer cooperatives, and associations in Marinduque.

## Where to go

- **Office of the Provincial Agriculturist (OPAg)** — Capitol Compound, Brgy. Santol, Boac.
- **Municipal Agriculture Office** — at your Municipal Hall; many municipalities keep their own machinery pools.

See the [Provincial Citizen's Charter](https://marinduque.gov.ph/citizens-charter/) for OPAg's official requirements and processing times.

## What to bring

- [ ] Valid ID and proof of farmer registration (RSBSA or municipal registry)
- [ ] Request letter stating the equipment needed, dates, and farm location — associations should request as a group where possible

## Steps

1. Ask your Municipal Agriculture Office what equipment is available locally.
2. Submit a request letter to the Municipal Agriculture Office or OPAg, well before your planting or harvest dates.
3. Wait for scheduling — equipment is usually rotated among barangays and associations.
4. Return the equipment on time and in good condition.

## Fees

Borrowing arrangements vary — some are free, others charge fuel, operator, or rental fees. Confirm the terms when you file the request.
