# Renew Permits and Pay Local Business Taxes

How to renew your Mayor's Permit and pay local business taxes in Marinduque — handled by your municipality's BPLO and Treasurer's Office.

Business permits in the Philippines are renewed **every January**. In Marinduque, renewals and local business tax payments are done at your **Municipal Hall** — through the Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO) and the Municipal Treasurer's Office.

## Who can use this

All businesses operating in the six municipalities: sole proprietors, partnerships, corporations, cooperatives, market vendors, and professionals with a business office.

## What to bring

- [ ] Previous year's Mayor's Permit and official receipts
- [ ] Barangay clearance (renewed)
- [ ] Community Tax Certificate (cedula)
- [ ] Declaration of gross sales/receipts for the previous year
- [ ] Valid ID
- [ ] Any updated inspection clearances (sanitary, fire safety) your municipality requires

## Steps

1. Secure or renew your **barangay clearance** at your barangay hall.
2. Go to your Municipal Hall's **BPLO** in January and file for renewal. Many municipalities run a one-stop renewal desk early in the year — watch your municipality's announcements.
3. Have your **local business tax assessed** by the Treasurer's Office based on your declared gross receipts.
4. Pay the assessed taxes and fees; keep all official receipts.
5. Claim your renewed permit and display it at your place of business.

## Fees

Local business tax rates and permit fees are set by your municipality's revenue code — ask the Municipal Treasurer's Office for the current rates and any penalties for late renewal.
