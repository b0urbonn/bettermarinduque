# Join Trade Fairs, Business Expos, or Tourism Promotions

How Marinduque businesses can join trade fairs, expos, and tourism promotions run by DTI, the province, and the municipalities.

## Who can use this

Local producers, handicraft makers, food processors, tourism operators, and other small businesses in Marinduque that want to exhibit products or promote their services.

## Where to go

- **DTI Marinduque** — runs business name registration and organizes trade fairs and business matching for local products. Ask at their provincial office.
- **Provincial Tourism Office** — at the Capitol Compound, Brgy. Santol, Boac. Coordinates tourism promotion and visitor information during the Moryonan Lenten Rites each Holy Week.
- **Your Municipal Hall** — for municipal-level trade events and fiesta promotions (see the municipal contact table on the [business permits page](/services/business/apply-for-barangay-clearance-and-mayors-business-permits)).

## What to bring

- [ ] Business permit and DTI/SEC registration
- [ ] Product list, photos, or samples
- [ ] Filled-out exhibitor or application form (per event)

## Steps

1. Watch for announcements of trade fairs and expos from DTI Marinduque, the Provincial Government's Facebook page (facebook.com/provincialgovernmentofmarinduque), and your municipality.
2. Contact the organizing office to ask about slots, fees, and requirements.
3. Prepare your products, display materials, and documents.
4. Confirm logistics — booth assignment, schedules, and any permits needed for food or live demonstrations.

## Fees

Participation fees vary per event — confirm directly with the organizing office.
