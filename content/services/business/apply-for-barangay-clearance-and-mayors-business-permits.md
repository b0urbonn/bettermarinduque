# Apply for Barangay Clearance and Mayor's Business Permits

How to register a new business in Marinduque — from DTI business name registration to barangay clearance and the Mayor's Permit at your Municipal Hall.

Business permits in Marinduque are issued by the **municipal government** where your business is located — not by the province. You apply at your Municipal Hall's **Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO)**.

## Who can use this

Anyone starting a business in any of the six municipalities — sole proprietors, partnerships, corporations, cooperatives, sari-sari stores, online sellers with a physical presence, and professionals with a business office.

## What to bring

Requirements vary by municipality, but typically include:

- [ ] DTI Business Name Certificate (sole proprietors) or SEC registration (partnerships/corporations)
- [ ] Barangay clearance from the barangay where the business is located
- [ ] Community Tax Certificate (cedula)
- [ ] Valid government-issued ID
- [ ] Proof of address — lease contract or land title/tax declaration
- [ ] Sketch or photos of the business location
- [ ] Filled-out unified application form (from the BPLO)

Some municipalities may also ask for sanitary, zoning, or fire safety inspection clearances.

## Steps

1. **Register your business name** with DTI Marinduque if you're a sole proprietor (partnerships and corporations register with the SEC instead).
2. **Get a barangay clearance** from your barangay hall.
3. **Apply at your Municipal Hall's BPLO** with the documents above. The BPLO may schedule inspections (zoning, sanitary, fire safety).
4. **Pay the assessed fees** at the Municipal Treasurer's Office.
5. **Claim your Mayor's Permit** and display it at your place of business.
6. **Register with the BIR** for your Certificate of Registration and official receipts.

## Fees

Fees are set by your municipality's revenue code — ask the Municipal Treasurer's Office or the BPLO for the current schedule. The [Provincial Citizen's Charter](https://marinduque.gov.ph/citizens-charter/) also lists fees for provincial frontline services.
