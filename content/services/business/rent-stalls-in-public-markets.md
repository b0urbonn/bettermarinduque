# Rent Stalls in Public Markets

How to apply for a stall in your municipality's public market in Marinduque — handled by the municipal government, usually through a market or economic enterprises office.

Public markets are run by the **municipal government**, not the province. Stall awards, lease rates, and market rules are set by each municipality — apply at the Municipal Hall where the market is located.

## Who can use this

Vendors and small business owners who want to lease a stall in a municipal public market in Boac, Mogpog, Gasan, Santa Cruz, Torrijos, or Buenavista.

## Where to go

Ask for the office that manages the public market — usually a **Market Office** or **Economic Enterprises Office** at the Municipal Hall.

## What to bring

- [ ] Filled-out application form for a market stall
- [ ] Valid ID and proof of residency
- [ ] Barangay clearance
- [ ] Community Tax Certificate (cedula)
- [ ] Business permit or mayor's permit, if already operating

Some municipalities prioritize applicants who are registered voters or long-time residents of the town — ask about eligibility rules.

## Steps

1. Go to the Municipal Hall and ask the Market Office whether stalls are available or if there is a waiting list or public auction/raffle for vacant stalls.
2. Submit your application and required documents.
3. If awarded a stall, sign the lease or award contract with the municipality.
4. Pay the stall rental and any required deposits at the Municipal Treasurer's Office.
5. Secure or update your Mayor's Permit before operating.

## Fees

Stall rentals and market fees are set by your municipality's revenue code — ask the Market Office or Municipal Treasurer's Office for current rates.
