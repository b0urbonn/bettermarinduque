# Report Illegal Dumping or Waste Management Violations

How to report illegal dumping, open burning of trash, and other waste violations in Marinduque.

## Who can use this

Any resident who sees garbage dumped in rivers, roadsides, vacant lots, or other unauthorized areas, or other violations of local waste rules.

## Where to report

- **Your barangay hall** — fastest for neighborhood dumping; barangay tanods can respond and log the incident.
- **MENRO / Municipal Hall** — for repeat offenses and violations by establishments.
- **Philippine National Police (PNP) municipal station** — if the dumping is ongoing, involves hazardous material, or you need an incident blotter.

## What to prepare

- [ ] Location (barangay, sitio, landmark) and description of the dumping
- [ ] Photos or video, if safe to take
- [ ] Date/time and, if known, who is responsible

## Steps

1. Document the violation safely — do not confront the dumper.
2. Report to your barangay hall or MENRO with the details.
3. For urgent or hazardous situations, call the PNP station or 911.
4. Ask for a reference number or blotter entry and follow up.

## Fees

Reporting is free.
