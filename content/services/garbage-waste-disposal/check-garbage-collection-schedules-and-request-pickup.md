# Check Garbage Collection Schedules and Request Pickup

How to find your garbage collection schedule and request special pickups in Marinduque — handled by your municipality.

Garbage collection is a **municipal** service. Schedules are set by each municipality, usually through the Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO) or the municipal engineering/general services office.

## Who can use this

Households and businesses in any of the six municipalities that need their collection schedule or a special pickup.

## Where to go

Ask at your barangay hall first — barangays coordinate collection routes and announcements. For missed collections or special requests, go to your Municipal Hall.

## What to bring

- [ ] Your barangay and street/sitio details
- [ ] Proof of residency (for special pickups or new service requests)

## Steps

1. Ask your barangay hall for the collection days and designated pickup points for your area.
2. For missed pickups or special requests, contact the MENRO or the Municipal Hall.
3. Follow the segregation rules — see [proper waste segregation](/services/garbage-waste-disposal/learn-proper-waste-segregation-and-disposal-methods).

## Fees

Regular household collection is covered by your municipality; special pickups may carry a fee set by the municipality — ask MENRO or the Treasurer's Office.
