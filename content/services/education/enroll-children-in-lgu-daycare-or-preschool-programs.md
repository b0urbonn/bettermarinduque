# Enroll Children in LGU Daycare or Preschool Programs

How to enroll young children in daycare (Child Development Centers) or public kindergarten in Marinduque.

Daycare services are run by the **municipal government** through Child Development Centers, usually under the Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO) and the barangay. Public kindergarten is handled by the Department of Education.

## Who can use this

Parents and guardians of young children in any of the six municipalities. Daycare generally serves pre-kindergarten-age children; kindergarten enrollment follows DepEd's age rules for the school year.

## Where to go

- **Your barangay's Child Development Center** — ask at the barangay hall for the daycare worker or Child Development Worker.
- **MSWDO at your Municipal Hall** — for daycare slots and schedules.
- **Your child's public elementary school** — for kindergarten enrollment under DepEd.
- **DepEd Schools Division of Marinduque** — Brgy. Isok, Boac, for division-level concerns.

## What to bring

- [ ] Child's birth certificate (photocopy)
- [ ] Proof of residency in the barangay
- [ ] Parent/guardian's valid ID
- [ ] Immunization or health records, if requested

## Steps

1. Visit your barangay hall or the nearest Child Development Center and ask for the enrollment schedule.
2. Fill out the registration form and submit the documents.
3. For kindergarten, enroll directly at your public elementary school during DepEd's enrollment period (announced before each school year).

## Fees

Public daycare and kindergarten are free. Some centers may ask for small contributions for materials — ask the center directly.
