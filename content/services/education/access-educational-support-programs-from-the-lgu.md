# Access Educational Support Programs from the LGU

Where Marinduque students and families can find school assistance — from provincial aid to school supplies, feeding programs, and national education assistance.

## Who can use this

Students and parents in Marinduque looking for financial aid, school supplies, or other educational support.

## Where to go

- **Provincial Capitol, Brgy. Santol, Boac** — the province provides scholarships and educational assistance; check the [Provincial Citizen's Charter](https://marinduque.gov.ph/citizens-charter/) for what's offered and how to apply.
- **MSWDO at your Municipal Hall** — municipal welfare programs, including support for indigent students and daycare-aged children.
- **PSWDO (Provincial Social Welfare and Development Office)** — at the Capitol Compound, Boac, for province-level welfare assistance.
- **DSWD** — national programs such as 4Ps (conditional cash transfer for poor households with school-age children) and AICS (Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations, which can cover educational needs). Apply through your MSWDO or the DSWD field office.
- **DepEd Schools Division of Marinduque** (Brgy. Isok, Boac) — for public school programs, Alternative Learning System (ALS), and division announcements.
- **DOLE/PESO** — employment and student job programs; ask at your Municipal Hall's PESO desk.

## What to bring

- [ ] School records or enrollment proof
- [ ] Certificate of indigency or residency from your barangay
- [ ] Valid ID of the applicant or guardian
- [ ] Any forms required by the specific program

## Steps

1. Identify the program that fits your need — tuition aid, supplies, feeding, or cash assistance.
2. Go to the responsible office (see above) and ask for the current application window and requirements.
3. Submit documents and keep the contact details of the handling officer.
4. Follow up — releasing schedules are usually posted or announced by the office.

## Fees

Government educational assistance is free to apply for.
