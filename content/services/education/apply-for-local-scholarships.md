# Apply for Local Scholarships

Scholarship and financial assistance options for students in Marinduque — from the province, local colleges, and national programs.

## Who can use this

Senior high school graduates and college students who are Marinduque residents, especially those from low-income households.

## Where to go

- **Provincial Capitol, Brgy. Santol, Boac** — the province handles provincial scholarships and educational assistance. Ask at the Capitol or watch the [Provincial Government's Facebook page](https://facebook.com/provincialgovernmentofmarinduque) for calls for applications. The [Provincial Citizen's Charter](https://marinduque.gov.ph/citizens-charter/) lists the requirements and processing steps for provincial frontline services.
- **Marinduque State University (MarSU)** — main campus in Tanza, Boac, with campuses in Torrijos, Gasan, and Santa Cruz. MarSU has a scholarship & financial assistance office. Tel (042) 332-2028, email info.office@marsu.edu.ph.
- **Other local colleges** — St. Mary's College of Marinduque (Boac) offers ESC grants, free SHS tuition, and scholarships for 4Ps grantees and top Grade 12 graduates; ask each school's registrar or scholarship desk.
- **National programs** — CHED/UniFAST and DOST-SEI scholarships accept Marinduque applicants (the DOST-SEI MIMAROPA scholarship system lists Marinduque scholars). Apply through the national calls.

## What to bring

- [ ] Report card or transcript of records
- [ ] Certificate of residency / barangay certificate
- [ ] Proof of household income (e.g., certificate of indigency) if the program is need-based
- [ ] Birth certificate and valid ID
- [ ] Filled-out application form

## Steps

1. Decide which program fits you — provincial assistance, school-based scholarship, or a national program.
2. Watch for official calls for applications (Capitol announcements, school bulletin boards, official Facebook pages).
3. Prepare and submit the documents before the deadline.
4. If shortlisted, attend any required interview or exam.
5. Keep copies of everything you submit and note the releasing schedule for the grant.

## Fees

Legitimate scholarship applications are free. Report anyone collecting "processing fees" for government scholarships.
