# Learn About Supplementary Activities for Schools in Your Area

Where to find clubs, competitions, cultural events, and enrichment programs for students in Marinduque.

## Who can use this

Students, parents, and teachers looking for activities beyond regular classes — contests, cultural programs, sports, and community events.

## Where to go

- **Your school** — most supplementary activities (clubs, intramurals, journalism, scouting) are organized at school level.
- **DepEd Schools Division of Marinduque** — Brgy. Isok, Boac. Posts division-wide contests, trainings, and circulars for public schools.
- **Marinduque State University (MarSU)** — campuses in Boac (Tanza), Torrijos, Gasan, and Santa Cruz host academic and community activities. Tel (042) 332-2028.
- **Provincial Tourism Office** — Capitol Compound, Boac. Shares information about cultural events, including the Moryonan Lenten Rites during Holy Week and the Bila-Bila Festival.
- **Marinduque Museum** — Brgy. Malusak, Boac, for heritage and culture-related activities.
- **Your Municipal Hall** — municipal fiestas and youth events (Mogpog's Kangga Festival, for example).

## What to bring

- [ ] School ID or proof of enrollment
- [ ] Parent/guardian consent for minors joining off-campus events
- [ ] Any entry forms required by the competition or event

## Steps

1. Ask your school adviser or coordinator which activities are open this school year.
2. Watch DepEd division and provincial government announcements for contests and festivals.
3. Register through your school or the organizing office before the deadline.

## Fees

Most school and LGU-run activities are free or charge minimal fees — confirm with the organizer.
