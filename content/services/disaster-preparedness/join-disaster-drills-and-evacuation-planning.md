# Join Disaster Drills and Evacuation Planning

How to take part in disaster drills and evacuation planning in Marinduque — through the PDRRMO, your municipal DRRMO, and your barangay.

Marinduque is an island province exposed to typhoons, and Mt. Malindig at its southern tip is a potentially active stratovolcano — disaster preparedness is handled by the **PDRRMO** (province) and each municipality's **MDRRMO**.

## Who can use this

All residents, schools, businesses, and barangay volunteers who want to join drills or help plan evacuations.

## Where to go

- **Marinduque PDRRMO** — Provincial Capitol, Boac. 0968-226-5727; Facebook: [PDRRMO Marinduque Official](https://facebook.com/PDRRMOMarinduqueOfficial).
- **Your municipal DRRMO** — at the Municipal Hall. Santa Cruz MDRRMO: 0920-455-4034; for other towns, ask at your Municipal Hall.
- **Your barangay hall** — barangays run their own drills and keep the local evacuation plan.

Numbers come from public directories and office pages (November 2026) — verify before relying on them.

## What to prepare

- [ ] Your household's own plan: meeting point, emergency contacts, and go-bag
- [ ] Willingness to volunteer — barangay response teams welcome residents

## Steps

1. Ask your barangay hall when the next earthquake, tsunami, or typhoon drill is scheduled (nationwide drills are held regularly).
2. Join the drill and learn your barangay's evacuation routes and the designated evacuation center.
3. Volunteer for the barangay disaster response team if you want a bigger role — ask the barangay captain or your MDRRMO.
4. Keep the emergency numbers handy — see [emergency response procedures](/services/disaster-preparedness/learn-about-emergency-response-procedures-and-evacuation-routes).

## Fees

Joining drills and volunteering is free.
