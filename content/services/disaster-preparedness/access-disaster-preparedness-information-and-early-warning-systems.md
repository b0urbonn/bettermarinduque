# Access Disaster Preparedness Information and Early Warning Systems

Where to get reliable disaster information and early warnings in Marinduque — official pages, local radio, and your barangay.

## Who can use this

All residents who want timely typhoon, flood, and other hazard warnings for Marinduque.

## Where to get information

- **PDRRMO Marinduque Official** — [facebook.com/PDRRMOMarinduqueOfficial](https://facebook.com/PDRRMOMarinduqueOfficial). Provincial advisories, bulletins, and preparedness information. Office: 0968-226-5727 (verify).
- **Provincial Government of Marinduque** — [marinduque.gov.ph](https://marinduque.gov.ph) and [facebook.com/provincialgovernmentofmarinduque](https://facebook.com/provincialgovernmentofmarinduque).
- **Your municipality and barangay** — MDRRMO advisories, barangay announcements, and megaphone/bandilyo warnings are the most direct local channel. Santa Cruz MDRRMO: 0920-455-4034 (verify).
- **Local radio** — Radyo Natin DZVH 105.7 FM (Boac), DWMD 104.5 FM (Santa Cruz), 100.1 FM (Torrijos), and Radyo Kamalindig DZNS 94.1 FM (community radio).
- **Marinduque News Network** — online local news.

## What to prepare

- [ ] A battery- or hand-crank-powered radio (power often fails during typhoons)
- [ ] Charged phone and the emergency numbers from the [evacuation procedures page](/services/disaster-preparedness/learn-about-emergency-response-procedures-and-evacuation-routes)

## Steps

1. Follow the PDRRMO and provincial government Facebook pages.
2. Note your municipality's local station and keep a working radio.
3. At the start of typhoon season, re-check your barangay's warning signals and evacuation center.
4. Share verified information only — do not spread unverified posts during emergencies.

## Fees

All of these information channels are free.
