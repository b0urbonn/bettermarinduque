# Learn About Emergency Response Procedures and Evacuation Routes

Emergency contacts and evacuation basics for Marinduque — who to call and how evacuations work in the province.

## Who can use this

Everyone in Marinduque — keep this page handy before typhoon season and know your barangay's evacuation center.

## Evacuation basics

- **Know your evacuation center.** Each barangay has designated evacuation sites — ask your barangay hall where yours is and the safest route to it.
- **Follow MDRRMO/PDRRMO instructions.** Pre-emptive evacuations for typhoons are announced through the barangay, local radio, and official Facebook pages.
- **Sea travel caution.** Balanacan Port (Mogpog) is the main sea link to Lucena; trips are suspended in bad weather — check with the shipping operator before traveling.
- **Mt. Malindig** (1,157 m), at the southern tip in Buenavista, is a potentially active stratovolcano — follow PDRRMO advisories for any volcanic activity.
- **Prepare a go-bag:** documents, water, food, flashlight, first aid, medicines, and a battery radio.

## Steps

1. Save the numbers above in your phone now.
2. Ask your barangay for the evacuation route and center for your household.
3. During typhoons, monitor official announcements and local radio; evacuate early when told.
4. After a disaster, coordinate with your barangay or MDRRMO for relief and reporting damage — see [relief goods or cash aid](/services/social-welfare/receive-relief-goods-or-cash-aid-during-disasters).
