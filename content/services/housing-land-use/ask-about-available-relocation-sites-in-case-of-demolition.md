# Ask About Available Relocation Sites in Case of Demolition

What to do if your household faces demolition or displacement in Marinduque — and where to ask about relocation.

## Who can use this

Households subject to demolition orders, eviction from danger zones, or displacement by infrastructure projects.

## Where to go

- **Your barangay hall** — register your household and ask about any ongoing relocation planning.
- **Your Municipal Hall** — the municipality coordinates relocation sites and works with national housing agencies; ask the Mayor's Office or MPDC.
- **MSWDO / PSWDO (Capitol Compound, Boac)** — for welfare assessment, affected-family listing, and available assistance.
- **PDRRMO** — if the displacement is due to a disaster or a declared danger zone (e.g., typhoon damage, volcanic risk around Mt. Malindig), the DRRM offices coordinate evacuation and temporary shelter.

## What to bring

- [ ] Proof of residency (barangay certificate, IDs, utility bills)
- [ ] The demolition or eviction notice, if you received one
- [ ] Household composition and certificate of indigency

## Steps

1. Do not wait for the demolition date — register early with your barangay and municipality.
2. Ask whether a relocation site exists and what the eligibility and documentary requirements are.
3. Undergo validation by the MSWDO or the designated office.
4. If no site is available yet, ask about interim assistance (shelter, financial aid) and get on the official beneficiary list.

## Fees

Consultation and registration are free.
