# Apply for Socialized Housing or Relocation Projects

How to ask about socialized housing and relocation in Marinduque — handled by your municipality, with welfare screening through the MSWDO/PSWDO.

## Who can use this

Low-income families, informal settlers, and households displaced by disasters or government projects in Marinduque.

## Where to go

- **Your Municipal Hall** — ask for the office handling housing or urban poor affairs (often the Municipal Planning and Development Coordinator or the Mayor's Office). Municipalities identify beneficiaries and manage any local housing sites.
- **MSWDO (municipal) / PSWDO (provincial, Capitol Compound, Boac)** — social welfare offices handle beneficiary assessment and referrals.
- **National agencies** — large housing programs come through national agencies (e.g., NHA); your municipality submits beneficiary lists, so registration starts locally.

## What to bring

- [ ] Certificate of indigency from your barangay
- [ ] Proof of residency and household composition
- [ ] Valid IDs of applicant and spouse
- [ ] Any demolition notice, disaster damage certification, or proof you are an informal settler, if applicable

## Steps

1. Register at your barangay and Municipal Hall as a potential housing beneficiary.
2. Undergo assessment/validation by the MSWDO or the municipal housing office.
3. Wait for the municipality's selection and awarding process — slots are limited and prioritized by need.
4. If awarded, comply with the terms (occupancy, amortization if any, no resale within the restricted period).

## Fees

Applying is free. Socialized housing may involve minimal amortization set by the program — ask the implementing office for exact terms.
