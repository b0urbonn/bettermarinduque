# Request Zoning, Land Use, or Building Permits

Where to get zoning clearance and building permits in Marinduque — issued by your municipality, not the province.

## Who can use this

Property owners and developers building, renovating, or changing the use of land or structures in any municipality.

## Where to go

All at your **Municipal Hall**:

- **Zoning / land use clearance** — usually the Municipal Planning and Development Coordinator (MPDC) or zoning officer.
- **Building permit** — the Office of the Building Official / Municipal Engineer.
- **Locational clearance for businesses** — often required as part of the Mayor's Permit application at the BPLO.

## What to bring

- [ ] Land title or tax declaration and lot plan
- [ ] Building plans signed by a licensed architect/engineer (for building permits)
- [ ] Barangay clearance
- [ ] Applicant's ID and contractor's license details, if using one

## Steps

1. Secure a **zoning/locational clearance** confirming your project fits the municipal land use plan.
2. Submit building plans to the **Office of the Building Official** for the building permit.
3. Pay assessed fees at the Municipal Treasurer's Office.
4. After construction, secure an **occupancy permit** before using the building — also required for business permits.

## Fees

Fees depend on project cost and are set by the national building code and your municipality's revenue code — ask the Building Official and Treasurer's Office for an estimate.
