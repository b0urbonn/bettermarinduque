# Report Damaged Roads, Bridges, or Drainage

How to report damaged roads, bridges, and drainage in Marinduque — and which level of government handles which road.

## Who can use this

Any resident reporting potholes, collapsed drainage, damaged bridges, or blocked roads.

## Who fixes what

- **Provincial roads and bridges** — **Provincial Engineering Office**, Capitol Compound, Brgy. Santol, Boac.
- **Municipal and barangay roads** — **Municipal Engineering Office** at your Municipal Hall, or your barangay for small sitio paths.
- **National roads** — DPWH (national agency); report through your municipality if unsure — they can endorse it.

See the municipal contact table on the [business permits page](/services/business/apply-for-barangay-clearance-and-mayors-business-permits). The [Provincial Citizen's Charter](https://marinduque.gov.ph/citizens-charter/) also lists provincial engineering services.

## What to prepare

- [ ] Location: municipality, barangay, sitio, and nearest landmark
- [ ] Photos and a short description of the damage and danger it poses
- [ ] Since when the damage has existed

## Steps

1. Report first to your barangay hall — they can act on small repairs and endorse bigger ones.
2. For provincial roads, report to the Provincial Engineering Office at the Capitol or through your municipality.
3. If the damage is dangerous (washed-out bridge, open excavation), warn neighbors and report immediately — for urgent hazards, call your municipal police station or 911.

## Fees

Reporting is free.
