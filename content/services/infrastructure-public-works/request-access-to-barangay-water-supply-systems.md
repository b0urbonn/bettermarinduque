# Request Access to Barangay Water Supply Systems

How to get connected to a communal or barangay water system in Marinduque.

## Who can use this

Households without a water connection, or those seeking service from a barangay water system or water district.

## Where to go

- **Your barangay hall** — barangay-level water systems (communal faucets, spring sources, pumps) are usually managed by the barangay or a Barangay Waterworks and Sanitation Association (BWSA).
- **Your Municipal Hall** — for municipal waterworks or referrals to the local water district/service provider.
- **Provincial Engineering Office** (Capitol Compound, Boac) — for province-assisted water infrastructure projects.

## What to bring

- [ ] Proof of residency in the barangay
- [ ] Valid ID
- [ ] Request letter or application form, if the association requires one

## Steps

1. Ask your barangay whether a communal system or BWSA serves your area.
2. Apply for a connection or membership with the managing association/barangay.
3. Pay any connection fee and agree to the association's rules and monthly dues.
4. If no system exists yet, petition through the barangay — water projects are often included in barangay and municipal development plans.

## Fees

Connection fees and monthly dues are set by the water association or provider — ask the BWSA officers or your barangay for the current rates.
