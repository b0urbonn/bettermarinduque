# Rent Stalls in Public Markets or Use LGU-Managed Cemeteries

Municipal-managed facilities in Marinduque — public market stalls and public cemeteries — and who to ask.

## Who can use this

Vendors seeking market stalls, and families needing burial plots or niche spaces in a municipal public cemetery.

## Where to go

Both are **municipal** services — go to your Municipal Hall:

- **Market stalls** — Market Office or Economic Enterprises Office (see the detailed guide: [Rent stalls in public markets](/services/business/rent-stalls-in-public-markets)).
- **Public cemeteries** — usually the municipal cemetery administrator or the Mayor's Office; ask at the Municipal Hall.

## What to bring

- [ ] For stalls: ID, barangay clearance, and business documents (see the [market stalls guide](/services/business/rent-stalls-in-public-markets))
- [ ] For cemetery use: death certificate, ID of the applicant, and proof of residency of the deceased, if required

## Steps

1. Ask the Municipal Hall which office handles the facility you need.
2. Submit the application and documents.
3. Pay the rental, lease, or burial fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office.
4. Keep the award, lease, or burial permit documents.

## Fees

Set by your municipality's revenue code — ask the Municipal Treasurer's Office or the managing office.
