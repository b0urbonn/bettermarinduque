# Use Public Facilities Like Gyms, Barangay Halls, or Multipurpose Centers

How to reserve public venues in Marinduque — barangay halls, covered courts, multipurpose centers, and municipal or provincial facilities.

## Who can use this

Residents, organizations, and event organizers who need a venue for meetings, sports, or community events.

## Where to go

- **Barangay facilities** (barangay hall, covered court, multipurpose hall) — reserve through your **barangay captain** or barangay secretary.
- **Municipal facilities** (plaza, municipal gym, civic centers) — reserve through the **Municipal Hall** (usually the Mayor's Office or General Services).
- **Provincial facilities** — e.g., the Moriones Arena in Boac; ask at the **Provincial Capitol**, Brgy. Santol, Boac (Mon–Fri, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM).

## What to bring

- [ ] Letter of request stating the event, date, time, and expected attendance
- [ ] Valid ID of the requesting person or organization head

## Steps

1. Submit a letter of request to the office that manages the venue — well before your date, since courts and halls book up during fiestas.
2. Wait for approval and confirm the schedule and any conditions (sound system, cleanup, curfew).
3. Pay any rental or deposit, and get the official receipt.
4. Return the venue clean and undamaged.

## Fees

Barangay facilities are often free for residents or charge a small fee; municipal and provincial venues set their own rates — ask the managing office.
