# Report Water, Air, or Marine Pollution

How to report polluted rivers or coastal waters, smoky air, fish kills, or other contamination in Marinduque — to your barangay, the municipality, DENR, or BFAR.

## Who can use this

Residents who notice fish kills, dead or sick marine life, discolored or smelly water, illegal dumping into waterways, or suspicious smoke or dust from a business or project.

## Where to report

- **Your barangay hall** — barangay officials can document the problem and refer it to the municipality. Coastal barangays often have bantay-dagat volunteers who monitor shoreline pollution.
- **MENRO / Municipal Hall** — for pollution from local businesses, markets, or public works in your town.
- **DENR PENRO Marinduque** (Boac) — the DENR's provincial office investigates water and air pollution and issues environmental compliance orders. DENR also has a hotline and email for pollution reports.
- **BFAR** — for marine pollution, red tide, or contaminated fishing grounds (coordinate through your Municipal Agriculture Office or the police if you can't reach BFAR directly).
- **PNP municipal station** — for an immediate public-nuisance complaint; ask for a blotter entry.

In an emergency, dial **911**.

## What to prepare

- [ ] Location (barangay, river, shoreline, or business address) and what you observed
- [ ] Photos or video, if safe to take
- [ ] Approximate time, and any company, vehicle, or vessel involved

## Steps

1. Note what you saw — color, smell, dead fish, smoke — and where.
2. Report to your barangay hall first if the source is local; they can refer it upward.
3. For ongoing or large-scale pollution, file directly with DENR PENRO Marinduque or BFAR.
4. Ask for a reference number or blotter entry and follow up.

## Fees

Reporting is free.
