# Report Illegal Logging, Quarrying, or Fishing

How to report illegal logging, quarrying, and illegal fishing in Marinduque — to DENR, BFAR, the police, or your barangay.

Environmental protection matters deeply in Marinduque: the 1996 Marcopper mining disaster halted mining on the island, and the province's coasts and forests are closely watched.

## Who can use this

Anyone who witnesses illegal logging, unauthorized quarrying or sand-and-gravel extraction, or illegal fishing (dynamite, cyanide, fine-mesh nets) in Marinduque.

## Where to report

- **DENR PENRO Marinduque** — Boac. The DENR's provincial office handles illegal logging and quarrying reports and permits verification.
- **BFAR** — for illegal fishing reports at sea or in municipal waters (coordinate through your Municipal Agriculture Office or the police if you can't reach BFAR).
- **PNP municipal station** — for immediate response and to file a blotter:

- **Your barangay hall** — barangay officials and bantay-dagat (sea watch) volunteers are often first responders for coastal violations.

Numbers are compiled from public directories and office pages (November 2026) — verify before relying on them. In an emergency, dial **911**.

## What to prepare

- [ ] Location (barangay, sitio, shoreline or forest area) and description of the activity
- [ ] Photos or video, if safe to take
- [ ] Time and any vehicle or boat details

## Steps

1. Document safely — do not confront offenders.
2. Report to the nearest office: DENR PENRO for logging/quarrying, barangay or police for active violations, BFAR for fishing.
3. Ask for a blotter entry or reference number and follow up.

## Fees

Reporting is free.
