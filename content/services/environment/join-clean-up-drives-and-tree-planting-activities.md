# Join Clean-Up Drives and Tree Planting Activities

How to join community clean-ups and tree planting in Marinduque — organized by barangays, municipalities, schools, and DENR.

## Who can use this

Residents, students, and organizations who want to volunteer for environmental activities.

## Where to go

- **Your barangay hall** — most clean-up and planting drives are organized at barangay level.
- **MENRO / Municipal Hall** — municipal environment programs and coastal clean-ups.
- **DENR PENRO Marinduque** (Boac) — the provincial environment office runs tree planting and reforestation activities.
- **Schools and civic groups** — DepEd schools and community organizations regularly hold drives, including during fiestas and the Moriones season.

## What to bring

- [ ] Work gloves, hat, water, and reusable container
- [ ] Sacks or tools if you have them (organizers often provide)

## Steps

1. Ask your barangay or MENRO about upcoming drives.
2. Register with the organizer if required, and show up at the assembly point.
3. Follow the coordinators' safety instructions, especially for coastal or riverside clean-ups.

## Fees

Volunteering is free.
